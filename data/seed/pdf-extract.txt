Strategic opportunities — NC ICT ecosystem 2025–2030
Six priority interventions to build a thriving digital economy in the Northern Cape province
1
Kimberley ICT Innovation Hub
Flagship co-working + incubation hub anchored by SPU and mLab NC. Transforms Kimberley into
the gravitational centre of the NC digital economy. Attracts DSI and TIA investment.
2
Karoo Data Economy Cluster
Data engineering, AI, and analytics startups in the Carnarvon–Kimberley corridor, anchored by
SARAO/SKA. Fibre infrastructure already in place as the platform.
3
Solar Valley Tech Incubator — Upington
GreenTech incubator co-located with solar operators in Upington. Smart grid ICT, energy
management software, and sustainability analytics serving the national renewable energy sector.
4
Rural Digital Economy Programme
Mobile-first platforms, e-commerce for farm produce, and digital government services for the
province's dispersed rural population. Aligned to SA's Digital Economy Master Plan (DEMP).
5
Mining 4.0 Incubation Corridor — Kathu
Startup programme co-funded by Kumba and Assmang SLPs at the Kathu CFERIS incubator. ICT
startups get a built-in corporate customer base and ring-fenced SLP funding from day one.
6
Women & Youth ICT Entrepreneur Track
Female founders and youth programme linked to mLab NC and SPU. Designed to attract national
and international NGO/DFI funding while addressing NC's inequality and unemployment.
