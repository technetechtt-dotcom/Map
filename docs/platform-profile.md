# SA ICT Ecosystem Map — platform profile

**Northern Cape pilot · national platform**

## Executive profile

The SA ICT Ecosystem Map is a governed public map and directory for South Africa's ICT, skills and innovation ecosystem. It helps provincial and national programme teams discover organisations, understand the evidence behind each record, and publish a current picture that partners can use.

Northern Cape is the depth-first pilot. The remaining eight provinces are structured as a national public-directory scaffold so the platform can expand without changing its operating model or data contract.

## The problem

Important ecosystem knowledge is distributed across spreadsheets, presentations, websites and personal networks. Programme leads cannot reliably answer who is where, what each organisation does, whether a record is current, or which gaps need a field visit.

## What the platform does

1. **Discover** — Searchable map and directory for ICT companies, hubs, campuses, funders, programmes and skills partners.
2. **Govern** — Draft, review, verify and publish workflow with source and status on the record.
3. **Coordinate** — Shared profiles, relationships, opportunities and geographic filters for programme work.
4. **Publish** — Public map views, national coverage, exports and a printable system book.

## Designed for

- Provincial economic development and skills desks
- Municipal innovation and digital-inclusion teams
- National coordinators and development agencies
- Incubators, hubs, universities and TVET partners
- Funders, programme operators and ecosystem researchers

## Trust model

The platform separates public discovery from private operational notes. Contributors maintain records within their scope, reviewers verify evidence, and administrators control publication. Role-scoped access, audit history, data-rights routes, encrypted secrets and backup controls support responsible operation.

## Rollout path

1. **Pilot** — Confirm priority datasets, owners and verification rules in Northern Cape.
2. **Operationalise** — Run contributor, reviewer and administrator workflows with measurable service levels.
3. **Scale** — Onboard provinces and partners through the same data contract, APIs and governance model.
