# Post-gates product growth roadmap

Do not prioritize these until `docs/launch-gates.md` gates 1–9 are green.

## Phase A — Discovery depth

- Richer opportunity matching (skills ↔ funding ↔ procurement)
- Funding and procurement discovery filters on national search
- Ecosystem network analysis (organisation relationships graph)

## Phase B — Intelligence

- Stakeholder dashboards (provincial vs national KPI views)
- Verification freshness and ingestion SLA alerts
- Export packs for provincial planning workshops

## Phase C — Engagement

- Saved searches and email digests (with POPIA consent)
- Programme / event calendar integrations
- Mobile-first field verification capture

Track delivery against measurable KPIs already wired in `/api/admin/data-quality` (province coverage, identity coverage, connector health).
